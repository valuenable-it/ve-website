Lenders

Life insurance is the 2nd largest financial savings asset class for Indians with total industry AUM at a staggering INR 53 trillion!

Unfortunately, despite the safety of this asset class, less than 0.5% is being used for the purposes of lending. This presents a huge untapped opportunity for lenders, which can be exploited with ValuEnable’s digital policy loan marketplace.

We leverage your existing digital infrastructure and combine the same with our proprietary insurer APIs to enable an end-to-end digital solution from sourcing the policies to assignment of policy (lien marking) in favour of the lender.
